Behind The Magic

From Image To Artwork

A trained deep-learning pipeline converts the visual statistics of your chosen style into a new artistic representation.

01. Choose

Select the photograph you want to transform and an artistic reference containing the visual style you want.

02. Understand

A VGG-based encoder extracts deep visual features from both the content and style images.

03. Transform

Adaptive Instance Normalization transfers style statistics and the trained decoder reconstructs the final artwork.
